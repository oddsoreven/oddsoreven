The record

Every round we publish, graded against the sharp close and the result. Bad rounds stay in.

How to read this

Picks: games where our number differs from Pinnacle's line by 2 points or more. Each pick is at Pinnacle's line when we priced the game, and is graded won, lost or push on the final score (overtime included). Units at Pinnacle's odds that moment: a win pays the odds minus 1, a loss costs 1.

Expected close: the least we expect Pinnacle's closing line to move our way. Reached or not, yes or no.

CLV: how many points the close moved our way from the pick's line. We took Partizan +5.5, Pinnacle closed Partizan +4.5: CLV +1.0.

Our line nearer the close: for every game, pick or not, whether our line was closer than Pinnacle's line at the time to Pinnacle's close.

The close is Pinnacle's last handicap before tip-off.
